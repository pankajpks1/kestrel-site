Public boards

Share a read-only board with people outside your project through a link, optionally with a passcode.

A public board is a read-only copy of a board that anyone with its link can open — investors, partners, a client — without a Kestrel account.

Turning it on for a project

Public boards are off until a project Owner enables them in Project Settings › Public Boards › Enable for Project. Without a passcode, a public board can be seen by anyone who has its URL, so review what a board shows before sharing it.

Making a board public

  1. Open the board and choose Share.
  2. Turn on the public link. Anyone who can edit the board can do this once the project allows it.
  3. Optionally set a passcode of 8 to 32 characters. Viewers enter it before the board opens; you can reset or remove it later.
  4. Copy the link and send it.

What viewers see

  • The board's title, its text and report cards in the same layout, for the last 30 days.
  • Nothing else: viewers can't open the reports, change them, or run their own queries.
  • A Report abuse link. Reports go to the project's Owners, who can deactivate the link or dismiss the report.

Stopping sharing

  • Deactivate a board's public URL from its Share window. A deactivated link never works again; making the board public later creates a new link.
  • Disable for Project in Project Settings removes the option for the whole project and deactivates every public link at once.
