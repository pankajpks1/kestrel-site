Project settings

Time period settings that shape your charts: the start of the week, quarter and weekend.

Project settings apply to everyone in the project. Admins can change them in Project Settings › Overview.

Time period settings

SettingDefaultWhat it affects
Start of WeekMondayWhere each week begins when a report is shown by week.
Start of QuarterJanuaryWhere each quarter begins when a report is shown by quarter.
Start of WeekendSaturdayThe first day of your weekend.
Duration of Weekend2 daysHow many days your weekend lasts, from 1 to 6.

All of these follow the project's time zone.

Weekends

Many products behave differently at the weekend, and the dips can hide the trend you are looking for. The weekend you set here is used by the Weekends chart option:

  • In an Insights report's chart options, set X-Axis › Weekends to Hidden to leave weekend days out of a line chart. The gap is drawn as a narrow break so the line stays readable.
  • You can also click a point on the chart and choose Hide Weekends or Show Weekends.
  • The option applies when the chart is shown by day. Weekends are visible by default.

Set the weekend to match your market — for example Friday and Saturday where that is the local weekend.
