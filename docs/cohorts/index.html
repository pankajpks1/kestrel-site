Cohorts

Define a group of users once and use it to filter, compare and track across reports.

A cohort is a group of users defined by what they did or who they are — "bought twice in the last 30 days", "signed up from India on Android". Cohorts are dynamic: the definition is saved, not the list of people, so membership updates as your data changes.

Creating one

  1. Go to Lexicon › Cohorts and choose + Create Cohort, or create one from a report's filter or breakdown.
  2. Add conditions: events users did (or didn't do), how many times and over what period, and user properties.
  3. Name and save it.

A cohort's conditions are built from events and properties; a cohort can't include another cohort.

Its size

A cohort's Count is everyone your project has seen — through an event or a profile — who matches the definition right now. The list refreshes the count every few minutes; Refresh Count in a cohort's ⋯ menu gets it immediately.

Visualize Trend charts the cohort's size over time; each point is its size at the end of that interval.

Using cohorts

  • Filter any report to people in (or not in) a cohort.
  • Break down a report by cohorts to compare groups side by side.
  • View Users lists the cohort's members, with their profiles and activity.
  • Export downloads the members as a CSV.

Managing cohorts

A cohort's ⋯ menu also offers Duplicate (as "Duplicate of …"), Verify (marks it as a trusted definition), Share and Delete.
