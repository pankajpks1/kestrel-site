Help Docs

How Kestrel's reports, data tools, boards and settings work.

Getting started

  • Setting up Kestrel — Send your first events, check they arrive, and open a board built on them.
  • MCP — Ask an AI assistant about your Kestrel data, and give Kestrel's Agent access to your other tools.

Reports

  • Measurements in Insights — What each choice in the Measuring menu counts, from unique users to aggregates of a property.
  • Segment limits — Why a broken-down report shows at most 3,000 segments, which ones you see, and how to reach the rest.
  • Custom buckets — Group a breakdown's values into ranges or named groups, automatically or your own way.
  • List properties — How properties that hold a list — tags, cart items — behave in filters, breakdowns and measurements.
  • Attribution — Credit each conversion to the touchpoints that led up to it, with the model of your choice.
  • Impact — Measure what a launch did to the behaviour you care about, by comparing the users who adopted it with those who didn't.
  • Signal — Find which events users did before reaching a goal are most tied to reaching it, and how often they need to be done.
  • Session Replay — Record visits to your website, watch them back beside their events, and see where people click.

Data

  • Lexicon — Your project's data dictionary: every event and property you track, what it means, and how it appears in reports.
  • Custom events — Combine or filter existing events into one event you can use in any report.
  • Custom properties — Build new properties from formulas over the ones you already track — no code change, applied to all your history.
  • Computed properties — Properties Kestrel works out from a user's history — when they first appeared, how often they did something — for breakdowns and filters.
  • Metrics and behaviors — Save a metric or a funnel / retention definition once, and reuse it in any report.
  • Lookup tables — Upload a CSV to add extra details to a property's values — product IDs to names, plan codes to prices.
  • Cohorts — Define a group of users once and use it to filter, compare and track across reports.
  • Cohort sync — Send a cohort's members to another tool, once or on a schedule, and keep that tool up to date as users enter and leave.
  • Data pipelines — Export your events, user profiles and identity mappings to your own cloud storage every day, as files you can load anywhere.
  • Real-time event streams — Send events to your own web service within seconds of arriving, filtered and shaped the way you want.

Boards

  • Board templates — Start a board from a proven set of reports, mapped to your own events in a few clicks.
  • Your value moment — The one action that shows a user got what they came for, and how to pick it.
  • Engagement primer — What engagement measures, how to read it in Kestrel, and the reports on the Company KPIs board.
  • Retention primer — Whether people keep coming back, how Kestrel measures it, and the retention reports on the template boards.
  • Growth primer — How many people get value from your product over time, and the growth reports on the Company KPIs board.
  • Public boards — Share a read-only board with people outside your project through a link, optionally with a passcode.

Experimentation

  • Feature flags — Turn a feature on for some users and not others, serve different values to different people, and change who gets what without shipping code.

Automations

  • Agentic automations — Have Kestrel watch a metric on a schedule and send you a written digest of what changed.

Project settings

  • Project settings — Time period settings that shape your charts: the start of the week, quarter and weekend.
  • Sessions — How Kestrel groups each user's events into sessions, and how to change the definition.
