Your value moment

The one action that shows a user got what they came for, and how to pick it.

Your value moment is the event that tells you a user actually got value from your product — not that they opened it, but that it did its job for them. Several board templates ask for it, because engagement, retention and growth all read better when they are measured on the action that matters.

Examples

ProductA good value moment
Social appCreating a post
Streaming serviceWatching a video
Online storeCompleting a purchase
Messaging appSending a message
Lending appA loan being disbursed

How to choose one

  • It should be an outcome, not navigation. "App Opened" or "Page Viewed" happen whether or not the user was helped.
  • Users who do it should come back. If you are unsure between two events, compare their retention in a Retention report — the better value moment usually retains better.
  • It should happen at a natural rhythm. Daily for a messaging app, weekly or monthly for a finance app. That rhythm tells you whether to watch daily, weekly or monthly active users.
  • Pick one event. If several actions count, you can combine them into a single custom event and use that.

What to track first

If you are just starting, track three things well before tracking everything: a sign-up event, your value moment, and the few steps in between. With those three you can already build the funnel from sign-up to value, the retention of users who reached it, and your active-user trend.
